What is Premium 1mm Slim Braiding?

Please check this before your appointment — the difference from standard skill braiding

01Standard Skill Braiding

A knot method tied with a skill needle

A knot method that uses a skill needle to weave hair and pieces together. The knots are round and thick, and since multiple strands are gripped at once, cutting/breakage can be significant.

  • Large, round knots create a noticeable feeling on the scalp
  • Pulling sensation may occur
  • Original hair may fall out along with the piece during removal
  • Procedure time is short, but durability is relatively weak
  • Heavier feel means it tends to sag faster over time
02J.JOOHYUN's Premium 1mm Slim Braiding

No tools — only skilled fingertip technique

A method that uses no tools at all, worked entirely by hand. It is J.JOOHYUN's own technique that minimizes the drawbacks of conventional extension methods.

  • Almost no hair breakage
  • Clean removal
  • Head doesn't look bulky, for an even more natural look
  • 100% handmade, so the procedure may take a bit longer
  • Lightweight with excellent durability

Do you always need to thin out hair for extensions?

We lightly texturize so the boundary connects naturally

To make extensions look natural, it's important that the boundary where your own hair meets the extensions isn't visible.

Especially if there's almost no layering and the ends fall heavy, connecting extensions directly can create a visible line where they meet, giving the common 'extension look.'

That's why, before the procedure, we don't cut much length off your existing hair — we simply do light texturizing at the ends.

Once texturizing is done, it doesn't mean a large amount is cut every single time you get extensions. For retouches or re-procedures afterward, we check the current condition and lightly adjust only what's needed, so the amount cut naturally decreases over time.

Can I keep growing out my own hair while wearing extensions?

Yes, you can grow your hair naturally while maintaining extensions

Since only a light texturizing is done to the ends at the first procedure, we don't cut off much hair.

In fact, once extensions are connected, the overall length and volume are filled in, so you can naturally get through the awkward in-between length that's hardest to manage while growing out short hair.

Hair generally grows about 1–1.5cm per month, so if you keep extensions for 2–3 months before removing them, you'll notice your own hair has grown longer than at the first procedure.

Many clients use extensions especially during the tricky styling phase of growing out a bob or medium-length cut.

Even with repeated retouches or re-procedures, we don't cut your hair short each time — we lightly texturize only what's needed so your own hair and the extensions connect naturally.

With consistent care, you can keep your extensions while naturally growing out your own hair, without cutting it short in between.

What is a 'volume add-on'? Do I need it?

Depending on your hair condition and desired style, an add-on is often needed for the best finish

A single basic pack of 80 strands (1 set) may not be enough to fully express the overall connection or volume. The extra extensions added in this case are called a 'volume add-on.'

An add-on isn't only needed for clients who already have thick hair.

The shorter your existing hair, the heavier your ends, the more uneven your hair length from damage, and the bigger the gap between your current length and the desired finished length — the more volume may be needed for a natural connection.

Especially with a bob, where the length difference between your own hair and the extensions is large, an add-on is often needed to blend the boundary naturally.

The amount of add-on needed varies for each client based on hair length, thickness, cut condition, and desired style, so it's hard to fix an exact quantity before the procedure.

When you visit, we first check your hair condition and give you an estimated add-on amount and price, then the final cost is determined based on the amount actually used during the procedure.

The price list also includes the average add-on cost, so please refer to it before booking.

Do I have to get a straight perm (magic straightening)?
POINT 1

If curl, perm, or C-curl remains, we recommend a straight perm beforehand

If your existing hair still has strong curl, perm, or C-curl, we recommend a straight perm before the extension procedure.

This is to reduce the daily hassle of having to straighten your short natural hair's curl separately after getting extensions, and so the long extensions connect naturally with your existing hair.

Bob-length or short hair especially tends to have volume or a C-curl.

If long, straight-falling extensions are connected on top of this, your short existing hair can stick out at the nape or the outer layer. It may also poke out when tied up or under a hat.

So calming down the ends of your existing hair beforehand makes it much easier to maintain a natural look after getting extensions.

POINT 2

Leaving the roots and straightening only the necessary part is enough

Since extensions naturally add overall volume and thickness, getting a straight perm doesn't mean the entire head needs to be fully pressed from the roots.

Usually leaving about 5–6cm from the roots and straightening mainly from the middle section (where extensions connect) to the ends is enough.

This way you keep the undetectably natural volume at the roots while calming down just the ends of your existing hair.

Also, since extension hair can naturally develop a C-curl or bend at the ends over time with daily wear, we recommend a straight (rather than C-curl) perm when treating your existing hair.

A straight perm also doesn't need to be repeated fully every single time you get extensions. If your hair was thoroughly treated before the first procedure, afterward you only need to touch up the regrown or necessary parts.

Note · When a straight perm is difficult due to damaged or bleached hair

If hair is heavily damaged from bleaching or frequent chemical treatments, a straight perm itself can be a burden on the hair.

Even in this case, the extension procedure may still be possible, but you may need to regularly style the curl or C-curl in your existing hair with a flat iron yourself so it blends naturally with the extensions.

Since the procedure is possible but daily styling can be a hassle, we don't strongly recommend this route for clients who find upkeep difficult.

If your hair damage is significant, please consult your designer first about whether a straight perm is possible.

Can I get extensions right after a straight perm?

If you left enough at the roots when perming, same-day extensions are possible

If you left about 5–6cm or more at the roots and applied the straightening solution mainly from the middle to the ends, same-day extensions are possible.

On the other hand, if you had a full straight perm all the way to near the roots, there may be strain on the hair and scalp near the roots, so we recommend resting for a while rather than connecting extensions right away.

In this case, we recommend waiting at least 3 days before getting extensions.

Even if you're perming to straighten strong natural curls or existing waves, please exclude the root area where possible and focus on straightening mainly from the middle to the ends, where it will directly connect with the extensions.

Can I tie up my hair after getting extensions?

Yes, you can tie it up!

However, getting extensions doesn't make your existing short hair grow longer too, so it depends on how long your own hair was before the procedure.

If your hair was already long enough to tie up naturally before the procedure, you can tie it up without much restriction afterward too.

On the other hand, if your original hair was short enough that side or back pieces used to fall out when tied up, that same short natural hair may still fall out between the extensions after the procedure.

In this case, simply pin down the short section lightly with a bobby pin or hairpin.

The same applies to a high ponytail.

If your existing hair was long enough to tie up high before, you can style it naturally that way after extensions too; hair that used to fall out due to being short may still fall out a bit afterward, so just pin it down.

Won't my head look bigger with extensions?

We use an approximately 1mm slim braiding method, taking your head shape and hair flow into account

J.JOOHYUN extensions use the 1mm slim braiding method, which makes the connection points as slim as possible.

Rather than simply attaching a large amount of pieces, we check your head shape, the thickness and flow of your existing hair, crown volume, and hairline to adjust the connection points and quantity.

So we consider the overall balance during the procedure to prevent the connections from clustering in one area and making your head look bulky.

However, if your existing hair itself has a lot of natural volume or bulk from curl or C-curl, extensions alone may not be enough to fully manage that volume.

In this case, coming in with your existing hair calmed down with a straight perm beforehand helps create a cleaner, more natural silhouette.

A strand came loose early on. Will it keep falling out?

One or two strands coming loose doesn't mean more will keep falling out

Braided extensions don't use melting bond or metal rings or any other separate adhesive material — each strand is braided by hand and secured with an elastic thread.

It's a sturdy method, but since it's hand-braided one strand at a time, durability can vary depending on your natural hair thickness, lifestyle habits, and how you shampoo and dry your hair.

So occasionally, one or two strands may naturally loosen or fall out after the procedure.

There's a lot of individual variation — some people lose a few strands within a month, while others don't lose a single strand for months.

Just because one strand fell out a week after the procedure doesn't mean the rest will keep falling out too, so please don't worry too much.

Please don't throw away a fallen piece — keep it, and bring it with you when you book an A/S appointment so we can check its condition and reattach it.

It's especially important to fully dry the scalp and connection points.
If moisture stays for a long time, the braided section can loosen or tangle, affecting durability, so please dry the connection points thoroughly after shampooing.

I see something like white dots in the braided section. Is that dandruff?

In most cases it's not dandruff, but the naturally shed hair ends remaining visible in the braided section

Human hair naturally sheds a certain amount every day as part of the growth-and-rest hair cycle.

Normally, shed hair falls away completely while shampooing or brushing, so it isn't very noticeable.

But while wearing extensions, some of your own naturally shed hair gets caught in the braided connection point and can't fall away right away, so it may remain there.

Over time, the white part near the root of this hair can look like a dot near the braided area.

When we untie the braid during a retouch or removal and comb it out, the hair that had naturally shed over time comes out along with it.

So this phenomenon alone doesn't mean you have dandruff, flaking, or hair loss.

If you occasionally see white spots in a noticeable spot like the crown or hairline, please don't forcibly pull them out.

Even already-shed hair is still fixed together with the braided section, so repeatedly pulling on it can reduce the amount of connected natural hair and loosen the braid.

If it bothers you, be careful not to disturb the connection point and only tidy up the visible part, or come in to have it checked.

Won't extensions cause hair loss?
POINT 1

J.JOOHYUN uses about 1mm slim braiding to reduce strain on your hair and scalp

There are several types of extensions — silicone, ring, bonded, braided, and more.

At J.JOOHYUN, we mainly use the 1mm slim braiding method rather than bond or metal rings.

We make the connection points as small and light as possible to reduce the sense of a foreign object and everyday burden, and carefully adjust the amount and location of connected hair to match your existing hair thickness and scalp condition.

That said, extensions are still a procedure that connects extra hair to your own, so how much strain you feel can vary by individual depending on how long you keep them, the amount used, your scalp and hair condition, and your care routine.

If your scalp is sensitive, you're experiencing hair loss, or your hair is very thin and weak, we recommend a thorough consultation before the procedure.

POINT 2

Seeing a lot of hair when removing extensions may just be naturally shed hair appearing all at once

Human hair naturally sheds every day as part of the hair cycle.

Normally you don't notice the amount much because it falls out little by little while shampooing or brushing.

But while wearing extensions, some already naturally shed hair gets caught in the braided connection points and can't fall away right away.

If you keep them for 2–3 months in this state and then remove the extensions, the hair that should have fallen out gradually comes out all at once, so it can feel like far more hair is suddenly falling out than actually is.

So it's hard to conclude that extensions caused all your hair loss just because you see a lot of hair when removing them.

That said, if noticeable hair loss continues beyond usual, or if you have scalp pain, redness, or other abnormalities, we recommend seeing a specialist regardless of whether you have extensions.

Can I dye, bleach, or perm my extensions?

Some colors can be dyed, but please decide carefully about additional chemical treatments

The extensions used at J.JOOHYUN can sometimes be dyed depending on the color and hair condition.

However, very dark pieces like Color No. 1 may have limits on lightening dye, and results can vary depending on the color you want.

In particular, special-colored pieces may have already gone through multiple bleaching and dyeing processes during manufacturing, so additional bleaching or repeated dyeing can increase damage even further.

Perming or applying strong chemical treatments again to extension pieces can also increase the chance of dryness, damage, tangling, and hair loss, so we don't recommend it.

If you want additional dyeing or perming, we recommend first checking the current condition and color of your pieces with us before deciding.

How many strands are in one pack?

J.JOOHYUN's basic set composition and strand count depend on the product specification used

Since the amount of hair per strand and the piece thickness vary by shop, it's hard to compare overall volume just by 'how many strands.'

For example, the same 100g of extension hair could be made into 200 thin strands, or into 160 strands with more hair per strand.

So a higher strand count doesn't necessarily mean more overall volume.

If each strand contains more hair, adding the same 10 strands can result in a noticeably fuller actual volume added.

We'll give you the exact basic quantity and set composition for J.JOOHYUN at the time of booking and consultation, and we'll also check the base amount and any needed add-on together based on your hair condition.

How long do extensions typically last?

We recommend an average retouch cycle of about 2–3 months

After the procedure, as your own hair grows over time, the braided connection points that were originally close to the scalp naturally move further down.

The longer your hair grows, the more of a gap forms between the braid and the scalp, and newly grown natural hair can catch on your fingers or get tangled while shampooing or brushing, causing daily inconvenience.

Since this varies by individual hair growth speed and care routine, there's no fixed exact number of months.

Some clients maintain longer without a retouch with good care, but before the connection points move down too far, we recommend checking the condition and getting a retouch roughly every 2–3 months on average.

The extension pieces themselves can be reused multiple times through retouching if well cared for, and some clients use the same pieces for about a year.

However, actual usage period varies depending on how often you use a flat iron, your shampoo and care routine, and how much the pieces shed or get damaged.

I have questions about retouching
POINT 1

What is a retouch?

As time passes after getting extensions, your own hair grows and the connection points that were originally close to the scalp gradually move further down.

A retouch is a procedure where we fully remove the existing pieces, re-prepare the reusable pieces, and re-braid them back up close to their original position.

If you want to keep the same length as your current extensions, we reconnect them at the current length.

If you'd like to shorten the length slightly or change your style, we can adjust as needed after a consultation.

POINT 2

How do I know when it's time for a retouch?

It's time to consider a retouch when newly grown natural hair starts catching on your fingers while shampooing or styling, or when the connection points have moved down noticeably more than before and feel looser.

We also recommend a retouch when naturally shed hair becomes noticeable around the braided area, or when the connection points start to feel messy.

On average it's often around 2–3 months, but individual variation exists depending on hair growth speed and care routine.

POINT 3

How many times can I get a retouch?

If the pieces are in good condition, most clients get about 1–3 retouches.

Depending on how well they're cared for, more reuse is possible, and some clients use the same original pieces for about a year through repeated retouching.

However, since extension pieces are also exposed to flat iron and blow-dryer heat and repeated shampooing and brushing while in use, they can gradually shed or become damaged.

If the pieces have noticeably thinned out compared to the start, or the ends are heavily damaged and tangle frequently, we recommend replacing them with new hair rather than continuing to retouch.

POINT 4

How much does a retouch cost?

A retouch is done on the premise of reusing the extension pieces you already have.

J.JOOHYUN's retouch cost is 50% off the regular first-procedure price, and removal is charged separately.

However, if new pieces need to be added due to damage or reduced volume in the existing pieces, an additional cost may apply based on the amount of hair added.

Can I remove the extensions myself?

It's possible, but the safest way is to have them removed at the shop

Some clients remove extensions themselves due to time or distance constraints that make visiting the shop difficult.

For the removal method, please refer to the guide video on J.JOOHYUN's YouTube channel, or ask us in advance during your visit.

Since braided extensions need to be removed by undoing the tied connection point, you may need to use scissors when removing them yourself.

If you can't accurately tell your own natural hair apart from the tied thread and cut it, your natural hair could get cut along with it, so please be very careful.

If possible, we recommend getting help from someone else rather than removing it alone.

When removing, use one hand to accurately locate and hold the braided connection point, then carefully cut only the tied thread to loosen it.

In particular, if the connection point is hard to see or hard to distinguish from your own natural hair, please don't force the removal.

The safest and cleanest method is to visit the shop and have a professional remove it for you.

Do I have to shampoo standing up? Can I shampoo every day?

For about the first week after the procedure, we recommend shampooing standing up, like in a shower

Getting extensions doesn't mean you always have to shampoo standing up.

Right after the procedure, the braided connection points are located close to the scalp, so bending your head down a lot while shampooing can cause pulling, and repeated strain can cause the connection points to move down a bit faster.

So for about a week after the procedure, we recommend shampooing in a standing, shower-like position.

Once some time has passed and the connection points have settled naturally, it's fine to bend down and shampoo as usual.

You can shampoo every day.

What matters more than how often you shampoo is thoroughly drying your scalp and the braided connection points afterward.

Leaving hair wet for a long time or sleeping with moisture remaining at the connection points can cause tangling or odor, and can loosen the braid, affecting how well the pieces stay in place.

Rather than worrying too much about how often you shampoo, keep up your usual cleanliness routine, and after shampooing, make sure to dry the scalp and connection points 100% completely.

Hair Extension Care Guide

The lifespan of hair extensions depends greatly on how you care for them after application.
Follow the steps below carefully to keep them looking beautiful for longer.

01STEP 1 · Shampooing

For about a week after the procedure, it's best to shampoo while standing upright like showering, rather than bending your head down.

After that you can shampoo as usual, but if you keep bending your head down to wash, it puts strain on the braided sections and the bonds may come loose a bit sooner.

First, thoroughly wet your entire hair with lukewarm water.

If your hair is tangled, lightly apply rinse or conditioner mainly to the extension hair before shampooing to detangle it first.

At this point, rubbing strands together or scrubbing roughly can cause tangling, so gently smooth the hair along its direction with your fingers.

Then take a moderate amount of shampoo and gently massage your scalp to cleanse it.

It's important not to scratch the bonds with your nails or rub them hard.

Rinse thoroughly so no shampoo remains, then apply treatment or conditioner mainly to the hair strands.

Just like natural hair, extensions stay softer and shinier when you consistently supply moisture and nutrients.

02STEP 2 · Drying

After shampooing, don't rub your hair with a dry towel — instead, press gently to remove excess water.

Using two towels can help reduce drying time.

Once the hair is no longer dripping wet, apply essence generously from mid-length to ends, following the direction of the hair.

Using the dryer from the top down helps smooth the hair cuticle and calm the strands.

Cool air is best for drying, but if it's taking too long, alternating between cool and warm air is fine.

Just avoid using excessively hot air directly on the bonds for a long time.

Please remember this

It's important to fully dry your scalp and the extension bonds.

If moisture is left for a long time or you sleep with damp hair, the braided sections may tangle or develop odor, and the bonds can loosen, causing pieces to fall out.

03STEP 3 · Styling

1. Straight

First, calmly straighten the natural hair at the crown and outer layer that isn't connected to extensions.

Next, hold the natural hair and extensions together and align the overall flow for a much more natural connection.

Rather than taking large sections at once, straighten the extensions in smaller amounts more slowly than usual for a cleaner result.

Be careful not to let the flat iron touch the bonds directly.

2. Wave

Before adding waves, first smooth out the flow of your entire hair.

For the extension section, it's best to divide the hair into small sections and curl with a curling iron.

If you take large sections of extensions and natural hair together from the start, the connection line may look unnatural — so curl the extension section first, style the outer natural hair separately, then blend them naturally at the end.

After curling, hold the curl shape until the hair fully cools for longer-lasting waves.

Finally, blend the flow of the outer natural hair and the extensions together for an even more natural style.

3. Finishing

Before using a flat iron or curling iron, we recommend using an essence or hair product with heat protection.

This helps reduce heat damage, and applying a small amount of essence after styling helps reduce dryness and static while keeping hair shiny.

04Helpful Information

J.JOOHYUN Hair Extensions uses natural hair with minimal coating to reduce artificial shine and create a texture close to real hair.

Right after the procedure, the hair may feel unusually straight and smooth, almost like it's been straightened, since it's fresh from the packaging.

After one or two washes, though, the hair's natural texture emerges and it settles into a texture much closer to real natural hair.

As natural hair, slight differences in texture, thickness, or cuticle feel between strands can occur — this is a natural characteristic of real hair, not a defect.

If a piece falls out

Braided extensions connect hair by hand without using any glue, adhesive, or bonding material.

While this method holds relatively firmly, depending on your natural hair thickness, scalp condition, lifestyle, and care habits, some pieces may occasionally loosen or fall out after the procedure.

There's no need to worry too much if a piece falls out.

Please don't throw away a piece that falls out — just keep it as is.

We'll check its condition and reattach it during your after-service visit.

06Average Volume Addition

J.JOOHYUN doesn't do simple factory-style extensions — we create custom designs with a distinctive sense of design and color using the most comfortable 1mm Slim Braiding method. The thin 1mm knots prevent head bulk, discomfort, and scalp pulling.

Hair ConditionAverage Volume Added
Long hair below collarbone1–2 times
Layered bob with many layers1–2 times
Bob without layers2–3 times
Short bob / short cut3–6 times

The volume addition amount is determined by length, amount, presence of layers, head size, and curl level.

  • The recommended average volume addition is based on what looks natural, and can be adjusted through consultation based on your desired style and lifestyle
  • We never push or force excessive volume additions
  • Listed volume amounts are average figures — actual amounts may exceed what is listed
  • The shorter your length and the thicker your hair before extensions, the more volume is required (essential factor)
  • Length can vary depending on height
  • There may be slight differences in length and weight depending on the manufacturer
  • It's difficult to give an exact price without seeing your hair condition beforehand, so please refer to the volume table for an approximate estimate
  • After treatment, we'll calculate the exact price per strand based on the amount actually used
